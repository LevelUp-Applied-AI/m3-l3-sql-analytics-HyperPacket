# KPI Brief — Levant Tech Solutions

## KPI 1: Department Salary Efficiency
**Definition:** Average salary per employee by department (total salary ÷ headcount)
**Current Value:** Engineering: 78,166.67 JOD | Research: 69,750.00 JOD | Finance: 68,833.33 JOD
**Interpretation:** Engineering has the highest salary efficiency cost, indicating it is the most expensive department per employee.

## KPI 2: Project Staffing Ratio
**Definition:** Average number of employees assigned per project (total assignments ÷ total projects with assignments)
**Current Value:** ~6.2 employees per active project
**Interpretation:** Most projects are well-staffed, though 2 projects have zero assignments and may need resourcing attention.

## KPI 3: Employee Utilization Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of employees assigned to at least one project (assigned employees ÷ total employees × 100)
**Current Value:** 42 assigned out of 60 employees = 70%
**Interpretation:** 30% of employees are currently unassigned to any project, representing potential underutilization of resources.

## KPI 4: Hiring Growth Rate
**Definition:** Month-over-month change in new hires based on hire_date records
**Current Value:** Peak hiring in Jan 2022 (4 hires) and Jan 2024 (3 hires); steady ~1-2 hires/month otherwise
**Interpretation:** Hiring is consistent but slowing in 2024-2025, which may indicate a hiring freeze or headcount cap.
